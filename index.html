Wanted!

PET HUNT

The WANTED poster shows one pet. Somewhere in the crowd, that exact pet is hiding. Find it and tap it before the clock runs out!

Meet a fluffy white dog, a grey cat, a pink bunny, a round hamster, a purple fish and a bright red cardinal. Every level, the crowd gets bigger and trickier.

Common questions

Is my best level saved?
Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU and UK, use the Privacy settings link on the game's title screen. See the privacy policy.

Is there an account or login?
No. Just open the game and play.
