Privacy Policy

WANTED! PET HUNT

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Wanted! Pet Hunt is a free game supported by ads. This page explains what data is involved.

What the game itself stores

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or personal details are required.
  • The only thing the game saves is your best level. It is stored on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • The game does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Ads (iPhone and Android apps only)

The phone apps show ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language), and
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • iPhone: the app asks whether it may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier and you will see less-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EU, UK and other regions where consent is required: the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice with the Privacy settings link on the game's title screen.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. The browser version of the game has no ads and collects nothing.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact
